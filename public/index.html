Studio Notice

✦ How to use DreamMosaic

Quick start · 6 steps

From memories to mosaic

Follow these steps in order. You can reopen this guide anytime.

♡

01 / SECURITY KEYS

Create your private two-person vault

  1. Open Set up vault on the welcome/login screen.
  2. Choose Mint Pair of Keys. DreamMosaic generates an Owner key and a Partner key.
  3. Save the credentials backup using Download Keys. Store it somewhere private.
  4. Use the Owner key to unlock your account. The other person uses the Partner key.

Keep the Owner key private. Share only the Partner key with the person you invited, using a private channel. Never post either key publicly.

02 / SHARE ACCESS

Invite your partner

  1. Send the Partner key directly to your partner.
  2. Ask them to open the same DreamMosaic website on their device.
  3. They enter the Partner key on the unlock screen and tap Unlock.

If you rotate or revoke a key in Security Vault, the previous key may stop working. Share the new Partner key only with your partner.

03 / ADD YOUR PHOTO COLLECTION

Upload a ZIP of tile photos

  1. Unlock the vault and open Film Roll Tiles from the menu.
  2. Tap Import ZIP and choose your .zip file from Files or your device.
  3. Wait for extraction to finish and check that the photos appear in your tile library.

Tip: Use a ZIP containing JPG, PNG or WebP photos. A larger, varied collection (ideally 50 or more photos) gives the mosaic more detail and better colour variety. You can also use Add Photos or select a folder.

04 / CHOOSE THE MAIN PICTURE

Select the image the mosaic should reveal

  1. Open Craft Mosaic.
  2. Tap the main image upload area and choose your target photo (JPG, PNG or WebP).
  3. Check the preview. This is the large picture that should be visible when you look at the finished mosaic from a distance.

Choose a clear, well-lit image with a recognisable subject and good contrast for the strongest result.

05 / GENERATE THE MOSAIC

Build and inspect your artwork

  1. Make sure your target image is selected and your Film Roll has enough photos.
  2. Adjust the mosaic settings if you want to change tile density or the visual style.
  3. Tap Generate Mosaic and let the process finish.
  4. Inspect the result. If the main picture is not clear enough, try increasing tile density, adding more varied tile photos, or choosing a clearer target image, then generate again.

06 / DOWNLOAD & KEEP

Export your finished mosaic

  1. Open Loupe & Export after the mosaic is generated.
  2. Choose Download High-Res PNG (or the high-resolution export option).
  3. Find the PNG in your device's Downloads or Files app, then save or share it as you like.

The exported PNG is your finished mosaic image.

A NOTE ABOUT YOUR PHOTOS

The current browser version imports ZIP photos and keeps its tile library in that browser's local storage (IndexedDB). Keep your original ZIP as a backup. Importing on one device does not automatically copy the photo library to the other person's device.
